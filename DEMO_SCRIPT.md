# Notch Companion — Live Presentation Demo Script (3-Minute Walkthrough)

This script gives you an exact, time-tested sequence to present Notch Companion with 100% confidence.

---

### Pre-Demo Checklist (10 seconds)
1. Launch Notch Companion (`coucou.exe` or `npm run dev` / installed app).
2. Press <kbd>Ctrl+Shift+R</kbd> (or click **"✨ Restore Demo Data"** in **Settings → Privacy & Data**).
   - *Confirmation:* A purple toast appears saying *"✨ Demo Reset: Fresh seed data restored!"*
   - *Result:* Re-seeds clean tasks (including today & overdue), 4 habit streaks, focus baseline, and turns on Presentation Demo Mode.
3. Make sure webcam is connected.

---

### Act 1: The Ambient Notch & Ergonomic Overview (0:00 - 0:45)
- **Action:** Move your cursor to the top center of your screen.
- **Say:**
  > *"Meet Notch Companion. Rather than cluttering the screen or running as a distracting full window, Notch lives in an ambient, dark-translucent island at the top of your display."*
- **Action:** Hover over the notch to expand it. Click the Pin icon (<kbd>Ctrl+P</kbd>) so it stays open during your explanation.
- **Say:**
  > *"Right across the top, the Overview Strip shows what matters right now: your active task, live focus timer status, and your current posture calibration."*
- **Action:** Show the 6 navigation tabs (<kbd>1</kbd> to <kbd>6</kbd>):
  - Focus · Tasks · Habits · Wellbeing · Insights · Settings.

---

### Act 2: Focus & Tasks Integration (0:45 - 1:15)
- **Action:** Press <kbd>2</kbd> or click **Tasks**.
- **Say:**
  > *"Here are our tasks organized by Today, Upcoming, and Overdue. Notice the overdue badge highlighting missed deadlines without anxiety."*
- **Action:** Click the **🎯 Target** button on *"Review quarterly project plan"*.
- **Action:** Press <kbd>1</kbd> or click **Focus**.
- **Say:**
  > *"Setting a task as active instantly binds it to the Focus timer. Watch as we hit Start."*
- **Action:** Click **▶ Start** (or press <kbd>Space</kbd>).
- **Say:**
  > *"When the notch collapses, the compact pill stays alive showing our active timer and task name directly in our peripheral vision. Mochi puts on his working expression to keep us company."*

---

### Act 3: 100% Offline Posture & Facial Fatigue AI (1:15 - 2:00)
- **Action:** Press <kbd>4</kbd> or click **Wellbeing**.
- **Say:**
  > *"Now for the standout feature: real-time ergonomic posture and fatigue analysis that runs 100% locally on your machine with zero external network calls."*
- **Action:** Click **Turn Camera On**. Show your face in front of the camera.
- **Say:**
  > *"Notice the red indicator dot in the island letting you know the camera is active. MediaPipe runs locally in WebAssembly. We click 'Calibrate', sit upright, and Notch learns our natural baseline."*
- **Action:** Click **Calibrate**.
- **Action:** Slouch forward visibly or tilt your head.
  - *Because Demo Mode is active (<kbd>Ctrl+Shift+R</kbd>), the warning triggers in just 5 seconds instead of 20.*
  - *The screen shows the alert with Mochi's slouching reaction.*
- **Action:** Sit upright again. Watch the status instantly turn green (*"Good upright posture"*).
- **Action:** Click **Turn Camera Off**.
- **Say:**
  > *"And when you turn it off, all video tracks are stopped immediately—the hardware camera light turns off instantly."*

---

### Act 4: Daily Habits & Insights Dashboard (2:00 - 2:40)
- **Action:** Press <kbd>3</kbd> or click **Habits**.
- **Say:**
  > *"Habits compound daily. We have streaks and 7-day completion dots. Notice that missed days are NEVER auto-completed—yesterday's missed walk stays honest so habits remain authentic. Tapping 'Done today' immediately celebrates your streak."*
- **Action:** Click `Done today` on one of the habits. Hear the pleasant audio chime.
- **Action:** Press <kbd>5</kbd> or click **Insights**.
- **Say:**
  > *"Our Insights dashboard compiles measured activity versus estimates. Notice the clear labeling and the core philosophy at the bottom: 'Descriptive insights, not a measure of productivity.'"*

---

### Act 5: Privacy Guarantee & Wrap-up (2:40 - 3:00)
- **Action:** Press <kbd>6</kbd> or click **Settings**.
- **Say:**
  > *"Everything in Notch Companion is local. No accounts, no subscriptions, zero keystroke logging, and zero screenshots. In Settings, users have full control: launch at sign-in, notification frequency, hover sensitivity, and a one-click 'Delete All My Data' button that wipes all storage instantly."*
- **Closing:**
  > *"Notch Companion: healthy posture, clear focus, and true privacy in a single ambient desktop experience. Thank you!"*

---

### Presentation Keyboard Shortcut Cheat Sheet
- <kbd>Ctrl+Shift+R</kbd>: **Hidden Demo Reset** (instantly restores clean presentation state)
- <kbd>Ctrl+P</kbd>: **Pin / Unpin** Island
- <kbd>1</kbd> - <kbd>6</kbd>: Quick switch tabs (Focus, Tasks, Habits, Wellbeing, Insights, Settings)
- <kbd>Space</kbd>: Start / Pause timer (when Focus tab is open)
- <kbd>Esc</kbd>: Collapse Island
