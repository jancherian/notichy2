Engineered Around Three Core Pillars

Productivity tools that ignore physical strain lead to burnout. Notch unifies cognitive focus and physical health into one ambient experience.

⏱️

1. Deep Focus & Task Alignment

A precision Pomodoro-style focus engine that embeds your active objective directly into your peripheral vision.

  • Configurable work (25m) and break (5m) cycles
  • Active task binding in the collapsed notch pill
  • Drift-free timestamped interval persistence
  • Interruption counters and session logs
  • Responsive Mochi mascot animations
🧍

2. 100% Offline Posture & Fatigue AI

Local computer vision evaluates your ergonomics in real time without sending a single frame over the internet.

  • MediaPipe Pose Landmarker running on local WASM
  • Detects forward-head slouch and shoulder tilt
  • PERCLOS eye-closure and blink fatigue tracking
  • One-tap ergonomic baseline calibration
  • Zero cloud dependencies; instant camera privacy kill switch
🌱

3. Micro-Habits & Wellbeing Resets

Cultivate sustainable work patterns with authentic streaks and non-intrusive micro-interventions.

  • Honest streak tracking (missed days stay visible)
  • Visual 7-day completion history row
  • 4 guided breaks: 20-20-20 eye rest, stretch, posture, water
  • Focus deferral holds reminders during deep work
  • Daily summary with provenance provenance tags

🔒 Built on Uncompromising Local Privacy

Most modern AI assistants transmit your audio, screen, and camera frames to external server clusters. Notch was built from day zero to operate completely offline.

🚫 Zero Sockets

Zero telemetry, analytics, or pingbacks. Verified with 0 open TCP network connections.

💾 Local WASM Models

All vision inference executes directly in memory using pre-bundled WebAssembly and .task models.

📷 Zero Video Saved

Frames from getUserMedia are evaluated in volatile memory and discarded immediately.

🛡️ No Keystroke Logs

Notch never logs, listens to, or inspects your clipboard, keystrokes, or application windows.

System Architecture

Lightweight native desktop shell powered by Tauri 2 and high-performance WebAssembly.

🛡️ Windows SmartScreen Notice

Because Notch is an unsigned open-source hackathon project, Windows Defender SmartScreen may display a blue warning screen ("Windows protected your PC") on first launch.

To install: Click "More info" and then click "Run anyway". Notch is 100% open-source, contains zero trackers, and never accesses external networks.
